ABA Therapy

What is ABA therapy?

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is the gold-standard, evidence-based therapy for children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and other developmental differences.

ABA Therapy

ABA is a therapy based on the science of learning and behavior. It uses proven techniques — like positive reinforcement — to teach communication, social, and daily living skills, while reducing behaviors that interfere with learning and quality of life. ABA is endorsed by the US Surgeon General and the American Psychological Association as an effective treatment for autism.

The idea is simple: when a behavior is followed by something valued, that behavior is more likely to be repeated.

ABA Therapy with Journeys

At Journeys ABA, every child receives a treatment plan built around who they are — not a one-size-fits-all program. Every program is designed and supervised by a Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), and therapy is compassionate and play-based.

We listen, we observe, and we work closely with families and caregivers — providing training and support so progress continues beyond therapy sessions.
